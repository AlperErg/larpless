## 3. Using LarpLess

- **The labels are estimates.** Focus scores and subject labels come from AI models and rules running on your computer, and they can be wrong. Don't rely on them for anything important, such as decisions about grades, discipline, work or health.
- **Track only yourself.** Use LarpLess only to track your own studying, on a computer you're allowed to use. Don't use it to watch another person's screen or camera, such as a child's, a student's or an employee's.
- **Permissions are your choice.** LarpLess asks for Screen Recording, Camera and Notifications. It works without the camera, and you can withdraw any permission in your system settings.
- **Other software has its own terms.** Ollama, and the AI models LarpLess uses or downloads, are separate projects under their own licences and terms. They're listed in **Settings → About → Acknowledgements**.
