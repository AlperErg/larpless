Privacy.

Everything runs on your device using on-device AI models.

  1. Recordings are never kept

    Screen and camera recordings are analysed and deleted during an active session. They are never retained.

  2. Analysis is offline

    A study session is analysed completely offline using local AI models on your device, meaning your screen and camera recordings never reach the internet.

  3. Storage

    Only the analysis of what you did during your study session is stored on your device. No screenshots, camera recordings, or even window titles are stored.

  4. Stats only

    Only your score and its breakdown is shared when you choose to share your session.
